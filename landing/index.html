What it wins

Realized cost is 68.7% below allowing everything and 73.7% below the rules. It wrongly blocks 20 times fewer genuine customers than the rules and 3.3 times fewer than the tuned threshold.

What it pays for that

The tuned threshold is cheaper by ₹35,201 per 1,000 orders. Without guardrails AegisShift would cost ₹1,55,110, cheaper still. The guardrails avoid 8 wrongful blocks at about ₹9,211 each: a deliberate trade, not a win on cost.

What we disclose

The limits are part of the result.

New accounts carry more friction

Accounts under 30 days old see 36.1% friction, against 4.8–5.9% for older cohorts. Account age stays in the model because it is genuinely predictive. Newness alone can never trigger a block.

Synthetic, by design

A seeded world with hard negatives (households, hostels, refurbished phones) and label noise. It validates the architecture and the policy's behaviour, not real-world effectiveness.

Privacy at the boundary

Identifiers arrive already HMAC-hashed, and anything else is rejected. No names, IP addresses or pincodes are used as features, and the graph shows masked labels only.
